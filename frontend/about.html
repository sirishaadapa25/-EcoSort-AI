About EcoSort AI

An AI-powered platform that helps users identify waste categories and learn responsible disposal practices for more sustainable everyday decisions.

The problem
Improper waste segregation is common in colleges, homes, and communities. People may not know whether an item belongs in organic/wet, recyclable/dry, hazardous, e-waste, or general waste. Wrong segregation can contaminate recyclable materials and reduce the effectiveness of waste management.
The question
How might we use AI to help people correctly identify and segregate waste and provide appropriate disposal guidance so that waste management becomes more sustainable?
Who it is for
College students, households, teachers, community members, and anyone who wants basic waste-disposal guidance.
SDG 12 (primary)
Responsible Consumption and Production. The project supports responsible waste handling, recycling, reuse, and sustainable consumption.
SDG 13 (secondary)
Climate Action. Better waste management is intended to contribute to reducing environmental impact. The project does not claim to solve climate change.
How it works
The frontend sends your description to a Flask REST API. The backend passes it to an AI model with a prompt that limits answers to five categories and forbids inventing local rules, then checks the answer before showing it.
Built with
HTML, CSS, and JavaScript on the frontend. Python and Flask on the backend.
