How it works

  1. Describe the item

    Type what you want to throw away, in your own words. For example: used battery.

  2. AI analyzes it

    The description is sent to an AI model that picks one of five waste categories. If no model is connected, a labelled demo dataset is used instead.

  3. Get clear guidance

    You see the category, how to dispose of it, a sustainability tip, an explanation, and what the AI is unsure about.

EcoSort AI supports decision-making and does not replace your local waste-management rules.
