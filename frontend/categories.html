Waste categories

Five groups cover most household and campus waste. Bin colours follow the common green, blue, and red convention. Open this page from a result and the matching bin opens.

Organic / Wet

Food scraps and other material that breaks down naturally.

Banana peel, leftovers, vegetable trimmings

Recyclable / Dry

Clean, dry materials that can often be recycled. Acceptance depends on your area.

Plastic bottles, cardboard, glass bottles

Hazardous

Items that can harm people or the environment if thrown in regular bins.

Used batteries, chemical containers, paint

E-Waste

Electronic and electrical items that need special collection.

Old phones, chargers, earphones

General Waste

Items that don't fit the other groups and can't be recycled locally.

Used tissues, soiled wrappers
