Responsible AI

EcoSort AI is a decision-support and awareness tool. Here is how it is designed to be used responsibly.

Fairness
Every description is analyzed with the same approach, regardless of who is asking.
Transparency
Each result includes an explanation, not just a category. The result also says whether it came from an AI model or from the built-in demo dataset.
Privacy
No personal or sensitive information is requested. What you type is used only to produce your result and is not stored by the app.
Accuracy
AI-generated recommendations may not always be correct. Verify disposal rules with local authorities or official waste-management guidelines.
Human oversight
EcoSort AI does not replace official waste-management instructions. Local rules always take priority.
Environmental responsibility
The app avoids unsupported claims about the environmental impact of specific disposal methods.
